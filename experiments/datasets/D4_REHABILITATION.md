# D₄ rehabilitated. The rotation test refuted a claim SQND does not make

*Written 2026-09-12, from `RESULTS_d4_rotation.md` in this directory and
`Non_Abelian_SQND_Bond_2026_v4_1.md` in `sqnd-probe`. Nothing new has been run.
Section 4 is the part that would make any of this evidence, and it is unrun.*

---

## 1. The verdict as recorded, and what it was actually against

`RESULTS_d4_rotation.md` closes with "a clean, informative negative for the
D₄-symmetric framework", on the strength of Part B. The interior test found the
rotation-covariant chirality `d·q` at +0.003 with the rotation-breaking `d²−q²`
at +0.340, and concluded that "the fourfold `d·q` pattern is a corner phenomenon,
not a continuous 90° symmetry."

That conclusion is correct and the arithmetic behind it is not in question. What
is in question is the target. **SQND does not claim a continuous 90° rotation.**
It claims the opposite, in print, as its central revision.

`Non_Abelian_SQND_Bond_2026_v4_1.md` exists precisely because the earlier
continuous group failed. Its abstract revises the gauge group "from **SU(2)_I ×
U(1)_H** to **D₄ × U(1)_H**: a discrete classical non-abelian structure rather
than a continuous quantum one." Its Protocol 1 finding is stated as "state
transitions require semantic triggers, not continuous field rotation", and the
theoretical implication is written as supporting D₄ **over** SU(2) for that
reason.

So Part B refuted continuity. Continuity is what SQND abandoned in order to become
a D₄ theory. The negative is real and it lands on SU(2), which was
already discarded.

## 2. The two domains agree, and neither knew it

SQND's most specific structural claim is the **generator asymmetry**, which it
reports as a discovery rather than an assumption. D₄ has two generators
and they behave differently in kind.

| Generator | SQND, moral domain | This repository, risk domain |
|---|---|---|
| **s**, reflection | Exact. 100 percent correlative symmetry, N = 100 over five scenarios, described as "the empirical signature of a true symmetry, not an approximate one" | V₄, the two reflections σ_v and σ_p, "real and dominant". ρ_SD = −0.80 on the value axis |
| **r**, rotation | Discrete and gated. N = 220 over eleven levels, non-monotonic in release strength. Level 5 fires at 100 percent, level 6 at 0 percent, level 7 ambiguous at 55 percent | A corner phenomenon. Chirality B = 0.80 at the corners and 0.003 in the interior |

Read across the two columns, the rotation test did not refute SQND. It re-derived
SQND's own generator asymmetry in a second domain, from
choice data rather than from introspection, and filed the result as a refutation
because it was scored against continuity.

The two halves of the rotation test then say something sharper together. The
chirality is not weak everywhere. It is strong exactly at the corners, at 0.80,
and absent exactly in the interior, at 0.003. A symmetry that is absent would be
weak in both places. A symmetry whose generator is **gated** is present where the
gate fires and absent where it does not, which is what two numbers an order of
magnitude apart describe.

## 3. What this does not establish

Everything above is a reinterpretation of results already in hand. It is
discovery material and it is not evidence, for the same reason set out in
`stratification-thesis.md`. The reading was suggested by these numbers, so
scoring it on these numbers is fitting and reporting the fit.

There is a second reason for caution specific to this case, and the original
record states it without prompting. Part A is marked "load-bearing" and **Part B
is marked "suggestive"**, on two named grounds. The continuous coordinates for mixed
gambles are "a pragmatic construction", and pooling the adversarial Kahneman and
Tversky problems with normal CPC18 "can attenuate the `d·q` term". A
rehabilitation that leans on Part B leans on the weaker half of the test.

It should also be said plainly that the exact D₄ was already broken at the
corners, in Part A, the half the record marks as load bearing. The E component is
significant at χ² = 75.4 with p = 4 × 10⁻¹⁷ and V₄ wins on BIC. Whatever survives
here is not exact D₄ on the risk plane. It is a gated rotation on top of exact
reflections, which is a weaker and more specific claim than either SQND or the
recorded verdict.

## 4. The prediction that would make it evidence

The gated reading and the absent reading make different predictions about the
interior, and the difference is measurable.

If the rotation is **absent**, the interior chirality is zero everywhere, and it
is zero in every subset of the interior.

If the rotation is **gated**, the interior chirality is not zero. It is present
where a gate fires and absent, or opposite in sign, where it does not, and the
pooled estimate near zero is the average of those. This is the same attenuation
the Part B caveat already names, promoted from a caveat to a prediction.

> **Prediction G.** Declare a binary gate variable over the interior choice sets
> before any refit. Conditioning the interior fit on that variable recovers a
> chirality `|d·q|` in at least one cell that exceeds the pooled estimate by a
> registered factor, the two cells differ in sign or magnitude by a registered
> margin, and the pooled estimate is recovered as the mixture of the cells.

**What refutes it.** A chirality indistinguishable from zero in every cell of the
declared gate refutes the gated reading and leaves the rotation absent, with D₄
rejected for this domain on the original verdict.

**What makes it vacuous.** A gate chosen after looking at which split produces a
non-zero chirality proves nothing at all. The gate must be named, with its
justification, before the refit, and the registration must name one gate rather
than a family to search over.

**The gate to declare, and why.** SQND's gates are discrete linguistic triggers. The nearest object in risky choice is the certainty effect, which is
already the σ_p reflection of the V₄ structure and is the classic discrete
trigger of the domain. A menu containing a sure outcome is on one side of the
gate and a menu of two non-degenerate lotteries is on the other. This is fixed by
the stimulus and requires no fitting, which is the property that makes it
admissible. The second candidate, kept as a named alternative rather than a
second bite, is the adversarial split the Part B caveat itself identifies,
Kahneman and Tversky problems with β_EV < 0 against normal CPC18.

## 5. What to do

1. Register Prediction G with one gate, the registered factor, and the margin,
   before any refit. The registration names the gate and the falsifier together.
2. Run it on the existing interior data, since the gate is fixed by the stimulus
   and not by the outcome. Keep the verdict.
3. If it passes, the claim available is a gated rotation over exact reflections,
   in two domains, with the moral-domain evidence introspective and the
   risk-domain evidence behavioural. That is worth stating and is not exact D₄.
4. If it fails, the rotation is absent in this domain, the recorded verdict
   stands as written, and the generator asymmetry is a moral-domain result that
   does not transfer. Record that and stop.
