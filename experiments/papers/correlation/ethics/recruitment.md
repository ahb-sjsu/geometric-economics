**RECRUITMENT: THE PROLIFIC STUDY LISTINGS**

Both listings are posted only on Prolific. Prolific shows them to members who live in the United States, are fluent in
English and use a desktop or laptop computer. Prolific also shows the payment, the estimated time and the devices
allowed beside the text below.

**1. The pilot**

**Study name:** Choices about points for you and for a charity

**Description:**

Researcher: Andrew H. Bond, San José State University ([email], or send a message on Prolific).

In this study you choose between two options, many times. Each option gives points to you and points to a charity you
pick from five well-known US charities. Some options are sure, and some depend on a coin flip. At the end, one of your
choices is drawn at random and paid for real, at 5 cents a point: up to $5.00 to you as a bonus, and up to $5.00 to
the charity as a donation.

What you will do: choose a charity, read the rules, answer four questions about them, make 160 choices, and answer
four short questions at the end.

Time: about 42 minutes, in one sitting.

Payment: $8.40 for taking part, plus the bonus.

You need a desktop or laptop computer, with a browser window at least 1000 by 620 pixels. You must be 18 or older.
